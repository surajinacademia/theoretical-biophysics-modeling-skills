The subsequent visual revision removes redundant emphasis while preserving the
scientific construction. The binomial square remains red while it is regrouped and in the final expression; constants remain neutral ink. Removing separate shift/constant accents avoids suggesting new categories midway through the derivation. Equations, domains, operations, and the documented square-root annotation correction are retained.
