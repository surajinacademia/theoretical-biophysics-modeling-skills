## Changes

The original ball-lighting gradient, drawing scale, stroke hierarchy and lower curved callout are restored. A pale tint of Minimalist light blue (4) supplies the ball color; rust (1) replaces red mesh lines and neutral ink replaces black. The unavailable surrounding-document caption is omitted, and the callout reads “Computational grid” with “[Rectilinear]” underneath. The established frozen-plane transform correction remains; projection formulas, supplied angles, mesh samples and labels retain their meanings.

Restored from the archived source without modifying archive bytes. The shared generated profile supplies palette names, CMU text, real Computer Modern math and native Latex arrowheads. Original geometric design and depth mechanisms take precedence over generic flat styling. Standalone wrapping removes unavailable document context where needed.

The established renderer correction freezes each named working plane’s trigonometric coefficients when its transform is defined. This prevents later macros from collapsing distinct projections; no supplied angles or projection formulas change.
