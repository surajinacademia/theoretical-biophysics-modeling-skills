# TikZ Gallery Licensing

This gallery has mixed licensing. `manifest.json` records each example's title,
provenance, source URL, and license.

- The twelve examples sourced from TeXample are adapted under CC BY-SA 4.0:
  <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>.
- `behavioral-timescale-buckets` is adapted from Izaak Neutelings under CC BY-SA
  4.0: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>.
- `optimization-decision-flowchart` is adapted from Jesse under CC BY-SA 3.0:
  <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/>.
- `lab-curriculum-flow`, `secant-regression-geometry`, and
  `orbital-elements-3d-trajectory` are independent original examples under the
  repository's MIT license. Their linked upstream files were used only as
  high-level pattern references because no redistribution license was present.

`source.tex` is the curated source for each example. `figure.pdf` and
`figure.svg` are regenerated specimens. Retain attribution, change notices, and
applicable share-alike terms when copying or adapting licensed gallery material.
When those terms are unsuitable, identify the construction technique and redraw
from first principles.

Public packaging normalizes trailing whitespace and tab indentation in text
sources. This formatting change does not alter the rendered examples. The
vendored `tkz-linknodes.sty` and `PACKAGE-LICENSE.txt` files are preserved
byte-for-byte.

`tkz-linknodes-examples/tkz-linknodes.sty` retains its separate LPPL/GPL notice
in `PACKAGE-LICENSE.txt`.
